### Storing energy to make firewood burning more convenient

To take advantage of all the benefits of a modern, environmentally approved firewood boiler, the excess energy produced by the boiler must be stored. The Aqualux Teknik 1000 provides large-capacity thermal storage with a bivalent shunt.
