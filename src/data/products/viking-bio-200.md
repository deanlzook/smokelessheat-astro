### Self-Cleaning Pellet Burner

The Viking Bio 200 is a self-cleaning pellet burner designed for reliable operation with wood pellets. The self-cleaning feature keeps ash from building up in the burner combustion tube.

**Key Features:**

- Self-cleaning burner design
- Handles pellets with higher ash content
- Reliable long-term operation
