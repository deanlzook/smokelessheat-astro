### Wood Gasification Boiler with Lambda control

**Advantages of the Vedolux Lambda Models:**

- Firewood burning with ultimate efficiency
- Easy to use controller
- Accommodates 20" logs
- Simple boiler tube / flue pipe clean-out design

The Vedolux Lambda 350 boiler works through reverse combustion with the help of a suction fan. In addition, a lambda sensor (oxygen sensor) is installed in the boiler flue that constantly analyzes how the boiler is burning by checking the proportion of oxygen in the exhaust. In real time the control makes small corrections to the air mixture to enable this boiler to reach outstanding efficiencies.

The combustion chamber holds approx 3 cu.ft of firewood and accepts wood up to 20" long.

When properly used and maintained the Vedolux 350 boiler has an output of around 35 kW or 120 kBTU per hour. This boiler tested at 93% efficiency when tested by the Swedish Test Institute.

The Vedolux 350 must be installed with an accumulator tank. Minimum recommended storage is approximately 500 gallons.
