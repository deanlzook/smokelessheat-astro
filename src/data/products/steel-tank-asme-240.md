### New steel tank for thermal storage

The Manchester Steel Tanks are brand new and come with enough ports to easily make the connections to a wood boiler. They are ASME rated tanks. They are designed to be used upright but with a cradle option could be used horizontally. The sizes we stock are 240g and 400g.

These tanks are primed and are stored outside, as a result they may have some surface rust.
