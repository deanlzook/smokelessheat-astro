**4.5 kW Electric Immersion Heater**

These immersion heaters fit Teknik and Aqualux CU accumulator tanks, and are often used to supplement or provide backup or freeze protection to wood and pellet-firing systems. The element can be added to other accumulator tanks outfitted with an appropriate 2″ threaded port.

The 4.5 kW element offers approximately 15.4 kBTU heating capacity.
