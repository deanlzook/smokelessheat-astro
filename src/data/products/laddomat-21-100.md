The Laddomat 21-100 is a loading unit designed for wood-fired boiler systems with thermal storage. It controls the flow between the boiler and the accumulator tank to maintain proper boiler temperature and efficient heat transfer.

Includes insulation jacket. Suitable for use with Vedolux boilers and Aqualux / Teknik storage tanks.
