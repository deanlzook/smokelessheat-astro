### Insulated Thermal Accumulation / Storage Tank

**Storing energy to make firewood burning more convenient**

To take advantage of all the benefits of a modern, environmentally approved firewood boiler, the excess energy produced by the boiler must be stored. The Aqualux Teknik 750 provides thermal storage with a bivalent shunt for flexible heating system integration.
