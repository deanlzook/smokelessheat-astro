### Wood Gasification Boiler with Lambda control

**Advantages of the Vedolux Lambda Models:**

- Firewood burning with ultimate efficiency
- Easy to use controller
- Accommodates 20" logs
- Simple boiler tube / flue pipe clean-out design

The Vedolux Lambda 450 boiler works through reverse combustion with the help of a suction fan. A lambda sensor constantly analyzes the oxygen content in the exhaust and the control makes real-time adjustments to the air mixture for outstanding efficiency.

The combustion chamber holds approx 5 cu.ft of firewood and accepts wood up to 20" long.

When properly used and maintained the Vedolux 450 boiler has an output of around 45 kW or 160 kBTU per hour. This boiler tested at 93% efficiency when tested by the Swedish Test Institute.

The Vedolux 450 must be installed with an accumulator tank. Minimum recommended storage is approximately 600 gallons.
